Option C — Strikethrough Continuity (the half-erased mental model)

  • Border: solid card-border — same as live cards. NOT dashed.
  • Background: same card-bg. opacity: 0.85 dims the whole card.
  • Meal name: SAME size and weight as live cards (text-base, font-medium) but in text-muted with line-through. Direct visual mapping: "this is the meal you deleted, half-erased."
  • Action line: "Tap to undo · 3s" in terracotta below the name.
  • Countdown: 3px terracotta bar at bottom edge.
  • Hover: opacity returns to 1.0, border highlights to terracotta-500.
  • Mood: the card IS the deleted meal in transition — strikethrough is the universal "deleted but reversible" affordance. Cooked uses strikethrough too (line 169 in MealCard.jsx) so this echoes existing language.
  • Risk: in a stack with a Cooked card (also struck through), they could look similar. Mitigated by sage-50 background on Cooked vs card-bg + opacity on Undo.
