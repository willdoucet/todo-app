Option Y — Seamless (the buttons-belong-to-the-meal approach)

  • Body: same min-h-[100px], padding-bottom shrinks slightly to make room for action zone.
  • Action zone: NO divider, NO background change. Buttons appear directly below the meta row.
  • Buttons: 32×32 circles in sage / red — same as Option X.
  • Animation: max-height 0 → 48px over 180ms; padding-bottom expands with it.
  • Cooked badge: stays in the body above the buttons.
  • Mood: minimal chrome. The buttons feel attached to the meal — actions for THIS meal, not a separate region. Subtraction default.
  • Risk: on stacked cards with the card body fairly empty (just a name), the buttons can feel like they're floating in space — less anchored than with a divider.
