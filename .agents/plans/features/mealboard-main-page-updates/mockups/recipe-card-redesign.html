Design Notes

  • Grid: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(280px, 1fr)) — cards stay ~280-360px wide even with 1-2 items. They never stretch to 600px+.
  • Placeholder: Soft warm gradient (hashed from recipe name) with a tiny muted utensil icon at 18% opacity. No letters. No emojis. Calm and intentional.
  • Heart: Airbnb-style — SVG floats with drop-shadow, no background. Unfavorited = white outline. Favorited = solid #E06B6B. Hover scales 1.15×.
  • Hover actions: Edit/delete icons fade in top-left on card hover, matching the drop-shadow style.
  • Lift: translateY(-3px) with a soft ambient shadow on hover — subtle, not distracting.
  • Content: Name at 15px/600 with 2-line clamp. Metadata at 13px muted. Compact padding.
