Spaghetti Bolognese

⏲ 15m prep ⏲ 30m cook 👥 4

Classic Italian comfort food. Slow-simmered meat sauce over al dente pasta.

Ingredients

  • •1 lb ground beef
  • •2 cloves garlic, minced
  • •1 onion, diced
  • •28 oz crushed tomatoes
  • •1 lb spaghetti

Instructions

  1. 1.Heat olive oil in large skillet over medium. Add onion, cook 5 minutes.
  2. 2.Add garlic and beef, brown 7–8 minutes, breaking up meat.
  3. 3.Add tomatoes, simmer 20 minutes. Season to taste.
  4. 4.Cook spaghetti per package. Toss with sauce. Serve.
